My visit to the Biodome

Montreal, Quebec — October 20th, 2026

Welcome to the Biodome!

This was a school trip with my classmates. We explored the different ecosystems and saw the animals that live in each one. Walking between the areas felt like travelling to different parts of the world because the temperature, plants, and surroundings changed along the way.

I really liked the Insectarium which was a very buitiful place to learn about insects. There was also a room full of butterflies that flew around you! Very cool!

Throughout the visit, I took pictures of the animals and the places we explored. You can see some of those photos in the carousel beside this entry.

Overall this was super cool place to visit! Would reccomend coming, because its not just nature, they also have some space exhibits on the side which is cool!
