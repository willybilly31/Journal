Saskatchewan

Craik/Regina, Saskatchewan — End of July - Start of August, 2026

Welcome to Saskatchewan!

I went to Saskatchewan during the summer to see some family. My dad was born in Saskatchewan, and my grandparents still live there. I go outto saskatchewan every two summers and I always have a great time.

The scenery there is really nice, even if its really flat and desolate. In regina, the park around the legislative building was beautiful, and had ducks and geese swimming in the pond. The legislative building itself was also really nice, and had a lot of history behind it.

In the small town of Craik, I spent time with my grandparents. They own farmland and two little airstrips. While i was there i cut the grass on both airstrips, and it was really fun and satisfying to see the results of my work. Sadly, no planes flew in while I was there.

Overall, this was a fun trip. It really shows off how diverse and beautiful Canada can be.
